Wellness Specialist Suite

Open · 2 part-time renters · $500/mo (50% off 1st month)

This is a license/booking arrangement, not a lease — a revocable, non-exclusive license to a 75–100 sq ft suite at Grand River Ave, with no landlord-tenant relationship. Practitioners book Part-Time only, $500/month (50% off the first month), for an 8-month Booking Period, paid via Square with a matching refundable damage deposit due at signing. Access requires proof of professional liability insurance (TSC as additional insured), a current Michigan license, two references, a background check, and in-person onboarding. Termination requires 30 days' notice; early termination forfeits the deposit plus one month's fee.

Event Space Rental

$150/hr standard · $120/hr member

This agreement governs event rentals at The Sisters' Couch Wellness Hub, a 40-person-capacity wellness space in Detroit. Renters pay $150/hour (3-hour minimum) or a flat $150 for 2-hour photo shoots, plus a separate $150 refundable deposit, with the balance due 14 days before the event. Cancellations 14+ days out get a full refund; anything closer gets none. Alcohol is strictly banned, furniture can't be rearranged, and events must align with the venue's wellness mission — violations can mean immediate termination without refund.

Retail Vendor Program

75% vendor / 25% TSC · consignment

This is a Retail Vendor Agreement between The Sisters' Couch Wellness Hub (TSC), a Detroit, MI wellness retailer, and a vendor placing non-perishable, mission-aligned products on consignment in TSC's retail space for a fixed 3-month term (non-renewing, new application required each term). The vendor pays a one-time $150 non-refundable admin fee and TSC keeps a 25% commission on sales, with vendor payouts and sales statements issued monthly on the 15th; TSC handles all point-of-sale, sales tax, and display upkeep, while vendor sets pricing, keeps items stocked, and bears full liability for their products. TSC can terminate with 14 days' notice for non-compliance, vendor can terminate with 30 days' notice (deposit non-refundable), and unclaimed products after term-end are considered abandoned after 7 days.

Couch Collective Membership

$80/mo · 13 founding members

This is a founding membership agreement for The Sisters' Couch Wellness Hub's "Couch Collective," a healing-centered wellness community for Black women in Detroit, running August 2026–July 2027 at a locked-in rate of $80/month billed automatically via Square. Members get drop-in co-working access (Mon–Thu, 10am–3pm), one quarterly guest pass, priority/member pricing on events, and exclusive Couch Collective programming, all contingent on staying in good standing. Cancellation requires 30 days' written notice (no refunds for the current billing period), and there are strict confidentiality and community-standards clauses — violations can lead to immediate membership revocation without refund. It also includes a media consent section (opt in/out for photo/video use) and standard governing-law/terms-update provisions.
